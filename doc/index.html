EMAIL ALIAS PROXY · CLOUDFLARE WORKERS · OPEN SOURCE

Unlimited email aliases.
Reply without leaking your address.

Cutout receives mail for your domain, matches glob rules, and forwards to your inbox, Discord, or Telegram. Reply from any of them and Cutout sends it back from the alias.

Deploy guide ↗ How it works
STEP 01

Catch-all intake

Email Routing sends inbound mail for your domain to the Cutout Worker.

STEP 02

First-match rules

Ordered glob rules match the recipient and select destinations.

STEP 03

Multi-channel fan-out

Forwards to your real inbox, Discord channels, or Telegram chats.

STEP 04

Reverse-alias replies

Replies route back through D1 mappings so your real address stays private.

DESTINATIONS

Forward wherever you read messages.

Email Proxy with reverse-alias replies, or direct pass-through
Discord Channel messages with an interactive Reply button
Telegram DMs or group chats with native reply support
PRIVACY

Keep your real address off the web.

  • Invent aliases on the fly for any sign-up
  • Route mail to email, Discord, Telegram, or the built-in web inbox
  • Reply from your mailbox or chat client as the alias
  • Drop noisy or leaked aliases with a single rule
ARCHITECTURE

Single Rust Worker on your own account.

  • ›Rust + Elm compiled to WASM on Cloudflare Workers
  • ›First-match glob rules across multiple domains
  • ›Durable reverse-alias mappings in D1 and raw message storage in R2
  • ›Management UI and web inbox protected by Cloudflare Access

Self-host on Cloudflare Workers.

Free, open source, and AGPL-3.0 licensed. Deploy your own instance in a few minutes.
