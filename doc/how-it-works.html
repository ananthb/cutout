How it works

Cutout sits between the outside world and your real inbox, routing inbound mail and outbound replies across email, Telegram, and Discord.

Inbound flow

  1. Catch-all intake: Mail to any address on your domain triggers Cloudflare Email Routing's catch-all rule and invokes the Cutout Worker.
  2. Rule matching: Cutout evaluates your glob rules top to bottom (first match wins) across local part and domain.
  3. Reverse-alias minting: On a Forward match, Cutout mints a single reply+<uuid>@yourdomain.com reverse alias in D1 shared across all destinations on the rule.
  4. Fan-out & storage: The Worker dispatches the message to each configured destination (email, Telegram, Discord) and, if Store is enabled, saves the raw MIME message in R2 for /inbox.

Forwarding modes

Each email destination chooses between two forwarding modes per rule (no backend env var required). Both inject an X-Original-From header with the real sender's address.

Proxy (default)

Sends via Cloudflare Email Service (send_email). Sets From to your alias (with the original sender in the display name) and Reply-To to the reply+<uuid> reverse alias so replies route back through Cutout. Drops attachments and signed MIME.

Direct (opt-in)

Calls EmailMessage.forward(). Passes raw bytes through untouched (attachments, PGP, original From/Reply-To), so replying goes straight to the sender from your real address. Requires the destination to be verified under Email Routing › Destination Addresses (unverified addresses fall back to Proxy mode).

Replies

When you reply from a proxied email or a bot thread, Cutout looks up the original sender and alias in D1 and sends the reply via send_email with your alias as From.

  • Email: Replies go to reply+<uuid>@yourdomain.com, which re-triggers the Worker via Email Routing.
  • Telegram & Discord: Telegram uses native message replies; Discord uses a Reply button on forwarded messages. Cutout records tg:<chat_id>:<message_id> and dc:<channel_id>:<message_id> in D1 to map webhook/interaction replies back to the thread.

Components

  • Cloudflare Email Routing: Inbound catch-all trigger, Direct-mode forward(), and destination address verification.
  • Cloudflare Email Service: Proxy-mode forwarding and outbound replies via send_email.
  • Cloudflare D1 (DB): Stores routing rules, permanent reverse-alias mappings, bot reply contexts, and pending dispatches.
  • Cloudflare R2 (EMAILS) & Queues (RETRIES): Raw email storage for /inbox and queued delivery retries with a dead-letter queue.
  • Cloudflare KV (KV): Auxiliary key-value state.
  • Cloudflare Access: Guards /manage and /inbox. Public endpoints (/health, /telegram/webhook, /discord/interactions) verify bot secret tokens or Ed25519 signatures.
  • botrelay-rs: Shared Rust crate for the Telegram and Discord bot clients.
